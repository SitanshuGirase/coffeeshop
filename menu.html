Rituals

Each cup has a time. Each method has a purpose.

How We Decide

No Default

We don't have a "house blend." Every bean is roasted for a specific ritual. The Kalita gets a lighter roast. The espresso gets development. The cold brew gets time.

Temperature as Intent

Dawn drinks are served at 68°C — hot enough to wake, cool enough to taste. Dusk drinks drop to 55°C. Sweetness emerges as heat retreats.

Vessel Matters

Wide rim for aroma (morning). Narrow for concentration (afternoon). Thick ceramic for heat retention (dawn). Thin glass for visual ceremony (dusk).

No Takeaway After 17:00

Evening coffee is for staying. The ritual completes in the cup, in the chair, in the conversation. Take a bag of beans home instead.
