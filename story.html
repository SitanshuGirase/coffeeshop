Transit

GrainPro → Container → Portland

63 days at sea. Temperature logged every 4 hours. Moisture stable at 10.8%. No mold. No rehydration. The bag arrives smelling of dried mango and brown sugar.

Roast

Probat L12 · 12kg batches

Charge temp: 195°C. Drop at 202°C. Development: 1:45. First crack at 9:12. We roast Monday. Rest 72 hours. Bag Tuesday. Serve Wednesday. The flavor window is 5–14 days post-roast.
