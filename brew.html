Kalita Wave 185

Flat-bottom. Three holes. Even extraction. The most forgiving path to clarity.

Morning Light–Medium Roast High Clarity Low Risk

Our Recipe

Dose
18g
Water
270g (1:15)
Temp
93°C / 200°F
Grind
Medium-fine (sea salt)
Filter
Kalita 185 white
Target Time
2:30–2:45

Procedure

  1. 0:00 Place filter, rinse thoroughly with hot water. Discard rinse water.
  2. 0:15 Add 18g coffee. Shake gently to level. Create small divot in center.
  3. 0:30 Bloom: Pour 40g water in slow spiral. Start center, work outward. All grounds wet.
  4. 1:00 First pour: 110g total (70g more). Slow concentric circles. Maintain 2cm water level.
  5. 1:45 Final pour: 270g total (160g more). Same spiral. Finish at 2:15.
  6. 2:30 Drawdown complete. Remove dripper. Swirl carafe. Serve immediately.

Marina's Notes

  • Flat bottom = even flow. Don't pour fast to "catch up."
  • If drawdown > 3:00, grind coarser. If < 2:15, finer.
  • Water at 93°C extracts sweetness. 96°C extracts bitterness.
  • Rinse the filter. Paper taste is real. Your palate knows.
  • This method forgives. But it rewards precision.

Hario V60 02

Conical. Single large hole. Spiral ribs. High clarity, high demand. For the focused morning.

Morning Light Roast Highest Clarity High Skill

Our Recipe

Dose
20g
Water
300g (1:15)
Temp
94°C / 201°F
Grind
Medium-fine (table salt)
Filter
V60 02 white
Target Time
2:15–2:30

Procedure

  1. 0:00Rinse filter thoroughly. Discard water. Preheat dripper and server.
  2. 0:15Add 20g coffee. Tap to level. Center divot.
  3. 0:30Bloom: 50g water. Spiral out. 30s. No dry spots.
  4. 1:00Main pour: To 300g. Continuous slow spiral. Center to edge to center. Finish 1:45.
  5. 2:15Drawdown. Swirl. Serve.

Priya's Notes

  • Single hole = flow control is yours. Pour speed = extraction.
  • Spiral ribs prevent channeling. But only if you pour evenly.
  • Light roasts need higher temp. 94°C minimum.
  • If it stalls, your grind is too fine. If it rushes, too coarse.
  • This method exposes. The bean. The roast. The pourer.

Espresso · La Marzocco Linea PB

Pressure. Precision. 9 bar. 25–30s. The most variable method. The most rewarding.

All Day Medium–Dark Roast Body & Texture Highest Skill

Our Recipe

Basket
18g VST
Dose
18.5g ±0.1g
Yield
37g (1:2)
Time
27–30s
Temp
93°C
Pressure
9 bar

Procedure

  1. —Purge group. Dry basket. Distribute (WDT). Tamp level, 15kg pressure.
  2. 0:00Lock. Start shot + timer simultaneously.
  3. 0:05–0:08First drops. Dark, viscous. Mouse tails.
  4. 0:15Steady stream. Golden crema forming. Color lightens.
  5. 0:27Blonding begins. Stop at 37g. Serve immediately.

James's Notes

  • Espresso is a 4-variable equation. Change one, adjust others.
  • Sour = under-extracted. Grind finer / dose up / yield down.
  • Bitter = over-extracted. Grind coarser / dose down / yield up.
  • Channeling = uneven puck. WDT every shot. No exceptions.
  • We dial in at 06:00. Re-check at 11:00. Humidity changes everything.

Cold Brew · 18-Hour Immersion

Time replaces heat. No acid. No bite. Pure sweetness. Afternoon only.

Afternoon Medium Roast Sweetness Zero Skill

Our Recipe

Coffee
120g (coarse, French press)
Water
1L filtered, 4°C
Ratio
1:8.3
Time
18 hours
Temp
4°C (fridge)
Filter
Paper after metal

Procedure

  1. Night beforeGrind 120g coarse. Add to Toddy. Pour 1L cold filtered water.
  2. —Stir gently. All grounds wet. Cover. Refrigerate 18 hours.
  3. Next morningDrain through metal filter. Then paper. Bottle. Chill 2 hours.
  4. ServeOver one large cube. No dilution. Sip slow.

Diego's Notes

  • Cold water extracts different compounds. Sugars. Not acids.
  • 18 hours is the sweet spot. 12 = weak. 24 = woody.
  • Paper filter after metal removes fines. Clarity without sediment.
  • We use the Guatemala Natural. Chocolate. Cherry. Molasses.
  • Served 12:00–17:00 only. After that, order espresso over ice.

AeroPress · Inverted Method

Immersion + pressure. Versatile. Portable. The barista's travel companion.

Any Time Any Roast Balanced Medium Skill

Our Recipe (Championship)

Dose
16g
Water
220g (1:13.75)
Temp
90°C / 194°F
Grind
Medium (kosher salt)
Filter
2× paper, rinsed
Time
1:45 total

Procedure

  1. 0:00Inverted. 16g coffee. 220g water. Stir 10s. Cap with 2 rinsed papers.
  2. 1:00Flip onto cup. Press slow. 30s press. Stop at hiss.
  3. 1:45Swirl. Taste. Adjust next time.

Priya's Notes

  • Inverted = no premature drip. Full immersion control.
  • Two papers = zero fines. Clean cup. Rinse both.
  • Lower temp (90°C) = less bitterness, more sweetness.
  • This recipe won Portland AeroPress 2023. It's a starting point.
  • Change one variable. Taste. Repeat. That's the method.
